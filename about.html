Don't throw it away. Recycle it

Plstka started in Egypt's Nile Delta with one promise to households: the plastic, aluminium and old electronics you throw out still have value — so trade them for something you need.مترميهاش بعيد — دَوَّرها ليوم جديد

01The question

Why does value end up in the bin?

Across the Delta, households were throwing away water bottles, cans and broken phones every day — materials that factories buy by the tonne. The people who produced that waste saw none of the value, and most of it never reached a recycler.

What we believe

The principles behind the product

⚖️

Value belongs to people

The person who sorts the waste should share in what it's worth. Rewards aren't a marketing gimmick — they're the point.

📈

Measure everything

Sustainability claims without data are just marketing. Every kilogram we handle is weighed, logged and traceable.

🤝

Build with communities

We hire locally, work with informal collectors instead of around them, and design for the streets we actually operate on.

⚡

Make it effortless

If recycling takes more than a few taps, people won't do it. Convenience is an environmental strategy.

🔒

Earn trust

Clear pricing, honest reporting, and no inflated impact numbers. Our credibility is the business.

🌱

Long game

We optimise for a circular system that still works in twenty years, not for a quarter's headline.
