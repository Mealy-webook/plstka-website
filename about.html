We're building the future of recycling

Plstka started with a simple question: why do people pay to throw away things that still have value? Today we're an AI-powered recycling platform serving tens of thousands of households and businesses.

Our story

From one city in the Delta to a regional platform

We launched in 2021 in Egypt's Delta region, where recycling was informal, fragmented and invisible. Waste had value, but almost none of it reached the people who generated it.

So we built two things at once: a consumer app that rewards households for recycling, and a business platform that gives companies real data on the waste they produce. Both feed the same network of vetted recyclers.

Today Plstka operates across four cities, has recycled over 1,500 tons of material, and is expanding across the Middle East — backed by a $1M round to accelerate the rollout.

🎯

Our mission

Make recycling the easiest and most rewarding choice for every household and business in the region — and make its impact measurable.

🔮

Our vision

A circular economy where no recyclable material reaches landfill, and where the people who sort waste share in the value they create.

Milestones

How we got here

2021

Plstka is founded

Launched in Tanta with a small pilot: door-to-door collection for a few hundred households, coordinated over WhatsApp.

2022

The app goes live

We replaced the spreadsheets with a real product — scheduling, points and a rewards catalogue — and crossed our first 10,000 users.

2023

Expansion across the Delta

El-Mahalla, Mansoura and Shebin El-Kom came online, and we opened our first sorting facility.

2024

Plstka for Business

Factories and retailers asked for the same data households were getting. The B2B platform launched with IoT-tracked collection.

2025

$1M raised

We closed a seed round to expand into six more cities and invest in AI-driven route optimisation.

2026

Going regional

60,000+ users, 1,500+ tons recycled, and the first steps beyond Egypt.

What we believe

The principles behind the product

⚖️

Value belongs to people

The person who sorts the waste should share in what it's worth. Rewards aren't a marketing gimmick — they're the point.

📈

Measure everything

Sustainability claims without data are just marketing. Every kilogram we handle is weighed, logged and traceable.

🤝

Build with communities

We hire locally, work with informal collectors instead of around them, and design for the streets we actually operate on.

⚡

Make it effortless

If recycling takes more than a few taps, people won't do it. Convenience is an environmental strategy.

🔒

Earn trust

Clear pricing, honest reporting, and no inflated impact numbers. Our credibility is the business.

🌱

Long game

We optimise for a circular system that still works in twenty years, not for a quarter's headline.
