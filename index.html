01 About plstka

We are Building the Future of Recycling

Founded in 2021 in Egypt's Nile Delta, Plstka lets households swap plastic, aluminium and electronic waste for discount coupons on food, healthcare and transport — and runs the collection behind it with AI-optimised logistics.

Today more than 60,000 people across Tanta, El-Mahalla El-Kubra, Mansoura and Shebin El-Kom use the app, and over 1,500 tonnes of solid waste have been kept out of landfill.
