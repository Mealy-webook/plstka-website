About plstka

We are Building the Future of Recycling

Plstka is a leading AI-powered recycling platform in the Middle East region, transforming how waste is managed and monetized. Founded in 2021, we began in Egypt's Delta and are now expanding globally.

We combine a gamified mobile experience with a powerful B2B platform to optimize waste collection and empower micro-earners — all while accelerating the shift toward a circular economy.

FAQs

Your questions answered

Here are some of the most common questions to help you get started with plstka.

How does Plstka work?
You collect and submit recyclable waste through the app. Plstka contacts you with the right collection and recycling options, all while rewarding you for your contribution.
How do I earn rewards?
Request a pickup, and you earn points based on the type and weight of the recyclables we collect — redeem them for partner coupons.
What kind of waste can I recycle?
Plastic, aluminum and electronic waste — from bottles and cans to old phones and electronics.
Is Plstka available in my city?
We currently serve Tanta, El-Mahalla El-Kubra, Mansoura and Shebin El-Kom, with more cities coming soon.
Is the service free to use?
Yes — scheduling a pickup is completely free. You only gain rewards.
Can businesses partner with Plstka?
Absolutely. Our B2B platform helps companies manage waste and meet ESG goals — reach out to our team.
