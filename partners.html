01 Ways to partner

Three ways to work with us

🏷️

Reward partners

Offer a discount or voucher in the Plstka rewards catalogue. Your brand appears in front of users at the moment they've earned something — a genuinely positive context. You pay only for redemptions.

For restaurants, retail, clinics, transport & entertainment
♻

Recycling partners

Buy sorted, weighed and documented material streams through our marketplace. Consistent quality, verified volumes, and full chain-of-custody records for every load you take.

For licensed recyclers & processing facilities
🎓

Programs & institutions

Run recycling drives with schools, universities, NGOs or municipalities. We provide the logistics, the app, the reporting and the awareness materials — you provide the community.

For schools, NGOs, accelerators & government
