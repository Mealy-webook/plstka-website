Why join us

What we offer

Not ping-pong tables — the things that actually matter when you're doing hard work.

🌍

Work that matters

Every sprint moves real tonnage out of landfill. You can point at the number and know you changed it.

💰

Fair, transparent pay

Banded salaries reviewed twice a year, plus equity for every full-time role. We publish the band in the offer.

🏠

Flexible hybrid

Two anchor days in the Tanta office, the rest wherever you focus best. Operations roles are field-based by nature.

🏥

Health cover

Private medical insurance for you and immediate family, from day one.

📚

Learning budget

An annual budget for courses, conferences and certifications — plus time to actually use it.

🏖️

Real time off

21 days of leave plus public holidays, and a culture that doesn't message you while you're on it.

Life at Plstka

Small team, wide ownership

We're 38 people covering engineering, operations, sustainability and partnerships. That means scope: you'll own problems end to end, and you'll spend time in the field with the collection teams — engineers included.

  • Everyone does a ride-along in their first month
  • Written proposals over status meetings
  • We hire locally and promote from within
  • Arabic and English both work here

Our process

How hiring works here

Usually two to three weeks end to end. We tell you where you stand at every stage.

01

Apply

Send a CV and a short note on why this problem interests you.

02

Intro call

A 30-minute conversation about your experience and what you're looking for.

03

Practical

A paid take-home or a working session — real problems, never busywork.

04

Team & offer

Meet the people you'd work with, then a written offer with the band shown.
