01 The app

Recycling that pays you back

Select what you want to recycle, book a pickup, and earn points the moment our handler weighs your items. Redeem them for real discounts from brands you already use.

  • Transparent points based on type and weight
  • Tier levels — unlock better rates as you recycle more
  • Live impact tracker for CO₂ saved and waste diverted
  • City leaderboards and monthly prize competitions
