Quick answers

Before you write to us

How fast do you reply?
Support messages are usually answered within a few hours during working days. Business and partnership enquiries get a reply within one to two business days.
My pickup didn't arrive — what do I do?
Use the in-app chat: it's the fastest route because it carries your order details automatically. If you can't access the app, email support@plstka.com with your registered phone number.
Is Plstka available in my city?
We currently serve Tanta, El-Mahalla El-Kubra, Mansoura and Shebin El-Kom, with six more cities planned. Send us your city and we'll notify you when we launch there.
How do I get a business quote?
Email business@plstka.com or book a demo from the Business page. Quotes are based on site count and volume.
Can I visit your facility?
Yes — we run guided visits to the Tanta sorting facility for schools, partners and media. Email hello@plstka.com at least a week ahead.
