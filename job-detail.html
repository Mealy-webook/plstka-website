Senior Backend Engineer

📍 Tanta, Egypt🏠 Hybrid💼 Full time📅 Posted 2 days ago
Apply for this roleApply for this role

About the role

Plstka runs a physical network — vans, handlers, sorting facilities — coordinated entirely by software. You'll own the backend services behind that: pickup scheduling, the routing engine's data layer, points and rewards, and the APIs our business platform exposes to clients.

This is a hands-on senior role on a small team. You'll design systems end to end, and you'll spend time with the operations team in the field to understand what you're actually modelling.

What you'll do

  • Design, build and operate backend services in Node.js and PostgreSQL that handle bookings, collections and rewards.
  • Own the data pipeline feeding our route-optimisation engine — volume prediction, service-time estimates and daily route generation.
  • Build and document the public API business clients use to pull waste data into their ERP or SAP systems.
  • Improve reliability and observability: we run real vans on real schedules, so downtime has a physical cost.
  • Review code, shape technical direction, and mentor two mid-level engineers.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years building production backend systems, with meaningful ownership of a service you took from design to operation.
  • Strong SQL and relational modelling — most of our hard problems are data problems.
  • Comfort with async workloads: queues, scheduled jobs, and long-running computation.
  • Pragmatism about tradeoffs, and the judgement to know when a simpler solution is the right one.
  • Working English; Arabic is a plus for field work with the operations team.

Nice to have

  • Experience with vehicle routing, scheduling or other optimisation problems.
  • Exposure to IoT or sensor data pipelines.
  • Having worked somewhere the software controlled physical operations.

What we offer

  • Banded salary reviewed twice a year — we publish the band in the offer.
  • Equity in the company.
  • Private medical insurance for you and immediate family, from day one.
  • Hybrid working: two anchor days in the Tanta office, the rest wherever you focus best.
  • Annual learning budget, plus the time to actually use it.
  • 21 days leave plus public holidays — and a culture that doesn't message you during it.

How hiring works

  • Intro call (30 min) — your experience and what you're looking for.
  • Paid take-home — a real, scoped problem. We pay for your time.
  • Technical conversation — we walk through your solution together.
  • Team & offer — meet the people you'd work with, then a written offer with the band shown.

A note on fit: if you meet most of this but not all of it, apply anyway. We'd rather read your application and decide together than have you screen yourself out. We review every one.
